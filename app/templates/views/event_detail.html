About the Event

Welcome to Austin's premier outdoor Summer Music Fest 2026. This year, we're expanding our community reach by uniting over 100 artists, local culinary vendors, and volunteers under the gorgeous Austin canopy. This 3-day event features premium performance stages, highly accessible check-ins, local art galleries, and a centralized food truck square.
